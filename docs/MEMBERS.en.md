# Coffee with CoffeePy – for members

*[Deutsche Fassung](MEMBERS.de.md) · For printing and hanging next to the machine.
Everything else is in the [user manual](MANUAL.en.md#4-the-kiosk).*

**The rule:** tap the section on the left first, then hold your card to the marked spot. The
start screen is **Order**.

## Having a coffee

1. Hold up your card – the kiosk greets you, your favourite coffee is preselected.
2. Tap **Order now** – or just take your cup: when the red countdown runs out, your favourite
   coffee is booked.
3. Something else? Tap the sort, then **Order now**.
4. **Changed your mind?** Tap **Cancel** – nothing is booked.

With many sorts, yours come first under **For you**, the rest is behind **More sorts ›**. A jug counts as two cups.

## The first time

Hold your card to the reader on **Order**. The kiosk asks for your **name** – first name and
the first letter of your last name, e.g. *Anna B.*, or a short code like *AB* – and your
**favourite coffee**. **Save**, and you go straight on to your order. Any card with an NFC chip
works, often your company or transit card too.

## Checking your balance and paying

Tap **Pay**, hold up your card. You see your cups and your balance; what you owe is already in
the field. Enter what you paid, pick the payment method and tap **Pay** – or scan the QR code
and pay with your phone. **No coffee is ever booked here.**

## Bought something for everyone

If there is a **Purchase** on the left, that is where you book what you bought for everyone –
coffee, milk, cookies: tap **Purchase**, hold up your card, tap the item, enter the quantity and
the price you paid, **Book purchase**. The price is credited to you, like a payment. In the portal
it's under **Purchase**.

## Settings and your phone

Tap **Settings**, hold up your card: change your name or favourite coffee, **Save**. There,
**Link this phone** connects your phone – scan the QR code with its camera yourself (a WiFi code
next to it first). With no app and no password it then shows your balance, bookings and stats,
takes payments and spare cards, and **Download my data** gives you everything the device stores
about you.

## When something goes wrong

| What | What to do |
|---|---|
| Booked by mistake | Tell an admin – they correct it. |
| Card lost | Tell an admin; a new card carries on (in the portal under **Cards**). |
| Phone lost | Log it out in the portal on another device under **Devices**, or ask the admin. |
| The kiosk shows "We'll be right back" | An update is running – wait a moment, it comes back by itself. |
| A sign or the wrench at the top right | For the admin, not for you – coffee still works. |
